1. **Data Protection**: Full incorporation of the **Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023** and **NDPR 2019**, recognizing the **Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC)**.
2. **Defensible Tax Records**: Explicit standards requiring compliant records under the **Personal Income Tax Act (PITA)** and **Companies Income Tax Act (CITA)**.
3. **Statutory Remittances**: Contractual clarity on employer obligations for Pension (PRA 2014), National Housing Fund (NHF), and Employees' Compensation (ECA/NSITF).
4. **Tenant Data Ownership**: Clients retain full ownership and Controller status over their workforce data; Trustline operates as a secure Data Processor.
