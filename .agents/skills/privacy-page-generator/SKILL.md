# Privacy Policy Page Generator (Nigeria NDPR & NDPC Compliant)

When generating or updating Privacy Policy pages for Trustline or clients in Nigeria:

## Mandatory Nigeria Compliance Requirements:
1. **Governing Law**: Enforce the **Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023** and **Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) 2019**.
2. **Regulatory Authority**: Explicitly name the **Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC)** as the supervisory authority.
3. **Data Subject Rights**: List the statutory rights under Section 34 of the NDPA (Access, Rectification, Erasure, Objection, Restriction, Portability, and Complaint to NDPC).
4. **Lawful Processing of HR & Payroll Data**: Detail lawful bases under Section 25 NDPA for processing employee financial, tax (PAYE/TIN), pension (RSA PIN), NHF, and identity data.
5. **Breach Notification**: Detail the mandatory 72-hour notification protocol to the NDPC for high-risk breaches.
6. **Data Protection Officer (DPO)**: Specify DPO contact information (`dpo@trustlineprofessional.com`).
7. **Cross-Border Transfers**: Cite compliance with NDPC adequacy standards.

Refer to the companion skill: `nigeria-legal-compliance` for detailed clauses.
